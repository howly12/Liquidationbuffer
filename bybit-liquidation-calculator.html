Bybit Liquidation Calculator

Pre-filled with Bybit's own fee structure and funding interval. Maintenance margin is left for you to enter — it depends on your pair and position size, and we don't guess it for you.

Bybit's maintenance margin isn't a flat percentage of your position — it's a tiered rate with a deduction term: MM = (Position Value × MMR) − Maintenance Margin Deduction. BTCUSDT starts at 0.50% MMR for positions up to $2,000,000, then steps up per risk-limit tier. Every other pair has its own table. Check yours on Bybit's margin parameters page ↗ before trusting a number here.

Bybit's own position tab already shows this: the maintenance margin figure it displays includes an estimated closing fee, added directly to what's required to avoid liquidation — meaning the fee genuinely moves your trigger price closer to entry, not just your payout afterward. That's the opposite of Binance's isolated-mode behavior. See Liquidation price vs. bankruptcy price for why that distinction matters.
