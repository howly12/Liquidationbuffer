MEXC Liquidation Calculator

Pre-filled with MEXC's own fee structure and funding interval. Maintenance margin is left for you to enter — it depends on your pair and risk-limit tier, and we don't guess it for you.

MEXC is unusual for how high its leverage ceiling goes — up to 400×–500× on BTCUSDT for the smallest positions — which makes the naive "1/leverage" assumption even further from reality than on most other exchanges. MEXC's own margin ratio formula is MMR = (Maintenance Margin + Liquidation Fee) / (Position Margin + Unrealized PNL), with liquidation triggered once that ratio reaches 100% — so like Bybit, OKX and KuCoin, the fee is written directly into the trigger, not deducted afterward. BTCUSDT's lowest risk-limit tier is commonly cited around 0.15% MMR, but every tier and pair differs. Check yours on MEXC's risk-limit page ↗.

MEXC also runs per-pair "special rate" taker fee promotions that can sit well below its advertised standard rate — worth checking directly rather than assuming one number applies across every pair you trade. See Liquidation price vs. bankruptcy price for why the fee-in-trigger model changes the math.
