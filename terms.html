What LiquidationBuffer is

LiquidationBuffer is a free information tool: a calculator that estimates how far the price of a leveraged crypto position can move before it is liquidated, plus guides about margin, fees and funding. It has no accounts and never asks you to connect an exchange account or enter API keys. It is not investment, tax, legal or financial advice, and nothing on the site is a recommendation to open, close or size a position. A result is an estimate based on the numbers you enter.
